**Solution**

Let \(D\) denote the event “the sensor is defective” and \(M_i\) the event “the sensor comes from line \(M_i\)”.

Using Bayes’ theorem:

\[
P(M_3 \mid D)=\frac{P(D \mid M_3)\,P(M_3)}{P(D)}
\]

First compute the overall probability of a defect:

\[
\begin{aligned}
P(D) &= P(D \mid M_1)P(M_1)+P(D \mid M_2)P(M_2)+P(D \mid M_3)P(M_3)\\
&= (0.02)(0.50)+(0.04)(0.30)+(0.10)(0.20)\\
&= 0.0100+0.0120+0.0200\\
&= 0.0420
\end{aligned}
\]

Now compute the numerator:

\[
P(D \mid M_3)P(M_3) = (0.10)(0.20) = 0.0200
\]

Finally,

\[
P(M_3 \mid D)=\frac{0.0200}{0.0420}\approx 0.476190476
\]

Rounded to four decimal places:

\[
\boxed{0.4762}
\]

**FINAL ANSWER:** 0.4762
